## Certification Process
To earn your **Certified ScrumMaster (CSM)** credential, you must:
1. Attend a **CSM training course** conducted by a **Certified Scrum Trainer (CST)**.
2. Pass the **CSM online exam** with a minimum passing score.
3. Accept the **Scrum Alliance License Agreement** and update your profile.
